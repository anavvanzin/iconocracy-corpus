# Bibliography comparison protocol

## Search layers

1. Foundational works defining the field or method.
2. Recent scholarship that revises the debate.
3. Regional and language-specific scholarship often omitted by international surveys.
4. Primary legal, archival, visual, or institutional sources.
5. Critical or rival traditions that challenge the chapter's assumptions.

## Comparison matrix

For each source record:
- full verified citation;
- discipline and method;
- central claim;
- corpus or jurisdiction;
- relation to the chapter;
- useful concept or evidence;
- incompatibility or limitation;
- exact section where engagement belongs.

Never produce a decorative bibliography dump. A source belongs only if it changes a claim, method, context, or objection.
