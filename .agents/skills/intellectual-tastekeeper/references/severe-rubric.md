# Severe rubric

Score 0 to 5.

## Necessity
Does the artifact need to exist, or is it a polished restatement of familiar material?

## Claim
Is there a precise, consequential move that can be defended?

## Evidence
Are claims proportionate to documents, objects, citations, or observed features?

## Method
Does the method change what can be seen, or merely decorate the vocabulary?

## Structure
Does sequence create cumulative force rather than a list of competent parts?

## Concepts
Are key terms defined, stable, and historically appropriate?

## Originality
Does the artifact produce a specific relation, distinction, or problem rather than generic novelty language?

## Voice
Does the prose sound authored, situated, and rhythmically alive?

## Public clarity
Can the intended audience understand the stakes without being patronized?

## Editorial restraint
Has the artifact cut repetitions, atmospheric filler, and explanations that do not earn space?

### Gate rules
- Any 0 or 1 in claim, evidence, or method: reject.
- Two or more scores below 3: major revision.
- No scores below 3 but unresolved local faults: minor revision.
- Ready requires no unsupported factual claims and no category below 3.
