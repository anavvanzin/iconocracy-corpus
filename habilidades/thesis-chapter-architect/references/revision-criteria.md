# Chapter revision criteria

Score each area from 0 to 4:

- Necessity: the chapter has a unique role in the thesis.
- Claim: the central claim is specific and defensible.
- Architecture: section order creates cumulative force.
- Evidence: claims are proportionate to sources and objects.
- Visual analysis: images perform analytic work.
- Literature: sources are engaged, not displayed.
- Concepts: key terms remain stable or shifts are explained.
- Counterargument: a serious objection is addressed.
- Transitions: sections connect through reasoning, not chronology alone.
- Ending: the conclusion transforms rather than repeats the introduction.

Any score below 2 is a structural revision, not a copyedit.
