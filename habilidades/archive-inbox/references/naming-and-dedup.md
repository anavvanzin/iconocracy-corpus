# Naming and deduplication

## Filename pattern

Prefer:
`date-creator-short-title-source-or-id.ext`

Rules:
- Use ISO dates when known: `YYYY`, `YYYY-MM`, or `YYYY-MM-DD`.
- Use `undated` only when the absence of date is documented; otherwise use `date-unknown`.
- Keep identifiers or shelfmarks when they are evidentiary.
- Use short, stable, lowercase ASCII slugs for technical filenames.
- Keep the original title and diacritics inside metadata.
- Add derivative suffixes such as `-web`, `-crop`, `-ocr`, or `-translation`; never overwrite the master.

## Duplicate levels

- Exact duplicate: same checksum or byte-identical file.
- Technical duplicate: same content with different encoding, size, or compression.
- Documentary duplicate: same object reproduced from another source or edition.
- Related version: crop, annotation, translation, restoration, screenshot, or derivative.
- Visual resemblance: not a duplicate.

Record why two items were grouped. Never delete automatically.
