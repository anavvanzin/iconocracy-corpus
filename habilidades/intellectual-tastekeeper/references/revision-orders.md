# Revision orders

Prefer commands that change the artifact materially.

## Structural
- State the claim in one sentence.
- Delete the section that repeats the introduction.
- Move the strongest object or case before the literature review.
- Split documented history from speculative interpretation.
- Replace chronology with an argumentative sequence.

## Evidentiary
- Prove the transmission route.
- Add the missing primary source.
- Narrow the claim to the corpus actually examined.
- Identify the edition, date, collection, or jurisdiction.
- Mark the relation as exploratory rather than documented.

## Stylistic
- Replace the abstraction with the actor and action.
- Cut the sentence that merely announces importance.
- Remove one adjective from every overloaded noun phrase.
- Break the symmetrical paragraph rhythm.
- Keep the strange sentence; cut the generic explanation after it.

Always order revision actions by leverage.
