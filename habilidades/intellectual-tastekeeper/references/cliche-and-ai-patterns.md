# Cliche and artificiality patterns

## Generic academic signals
- Opening with broad claims about the contemporary world.
- Repeated phrases such as it is important to note, in this sense, not only but also, or invites us to reflect.
- Theory names appearing at paragraph endings as seals of sophistication.
- Three-part lists repeated with identical cadence.
- Abstract nouns replacing agents, actions, dates, and institutions.

## Generic cultural-copy signals
- Fascinating, iconic, powerful, timeless, thought-provoking, or must-see without explanation.
- The archive speaks, the image resists, the past echoes, or history comes alive.
- Recommendations based on fame rather than fit.

## Project-specific overuse
- blindfolds, ghosts, ruins, shadows, wounds, haunting, gaze, body, memory, power;
- Marianne or Justice used as universal female symbols without jurisdiction or chronology;
- Warburg reduced to constellation as a decorative synonym for grouping.

Do not ban a word mechanically. Flag it when the sentence would remain unchanged if the object, period, or jurisdiction were replaced.
