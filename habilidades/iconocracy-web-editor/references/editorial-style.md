# Editorial style

## Voice
- Intelligent, precise, visual, and readable.
- Slightly nocturnal in atmosphere, never theatrical.
- Scholarly without institutional sludge.
- Curious without false wonder.

## Avoid
- Generic openings about images having power.
- Claims that an archive speaks, haunts, or resists without analysis.
- Empty intensifiers such as deeply, profoundly, strikingly, or fascinatingly.
- Mechanical triplets and repetitive rhetorical questions.
- SEO headings that distort the argument.
- Explaining obvious visual facts while skipping difficult context.

## Prefer
- Concrete objects, dates, gestures, materials, and institutions.
- Short interpretive claims tied to evidence.
- Clear distinctions between fact, interpretation, and hypothesis.
- Titles with conceptual tension rather than academic subtitles by default.
