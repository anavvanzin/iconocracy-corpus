## Selection logic

The eight figures map one-to-one to the article's analytical sections, anchored to the corpus items that the WP itself analyses by name. The first six are obligatory (no §3–§7 case argument survives without its image); the last two — Bielefeld Notgeld and Indochina Piastre — are high-value for the contra-cases (§4.4 valve and §7 colonial-comparison-with-France).

## Source plan (per figure)

### Fig. 1 — Educational $5 Silver Certificate (Friedberg 268), 1896

- **SCOUT ID:** US-EDUC-1896-05
- **Primary source:** Smithsonian National Museum of American History
- **URL:** https://americanhistory.si.edu/collections/object/nmah_1816788
- **Copyright:** Smithsonian "Open Access" — CC0 for most NMAH numismatic items (verify per object)
- **Resolution:** high-res available on Smithsonian portal
- **Proposed caption:** "Five-dollar 'Educational Series' Silver Certificate (Friedberg 268), 1896. Centre vignette: 'Electricity Presenting Light to the World' by Walter Shirlaw, engraved by G.F.C. Smillie. *Endurecimento* score 1.4 (LOW-MEDIUM); dessexualization indicator: 0 (bare-breasted figure). Withdrawn from circulation 1899. Smithsonian NMAH."
- **Alt source if Smithsonian metadata fails:** Bureau of Engraving and Printing historical archive; FRBSF museum.

### Fig. 2 — Semeuse 1 Franc silver coin (Roty), 1898

- **SCOUT ID:** present in corpus but not isolated by exact title match — verify under "Semeuse 1 Franc silver coin (Oscar Roty)" in coded items (score 2.50, see Appendix A row 8)
- **Primary source:** Monnaie de Paris archives or BnF Gallica
- **URL candidates:** https://gallica.bnf.fr/ark:/ (specific Roty coin photo); fallback Wikimedia Commons "1 franc Semeuse" entries (multiple uploads under PD-FRANCE-PRE1923 or CC-BY).
- **Copyright:** pre-1923 design → US public domain by 95-year rule; France: design subject to author's life + 70 (Roty d. 1911, in PD since 1982).
- **Proposed caption:** "1 Franc Semeuse silver coin, France, 1898. Obverse design by Louis-Oscar Roty (1846–1911). *Endurecimento* score 2.50; dessexualization indicator: 3 (fully clothed, dynamic posture). In continuous use 1898–1959; redesigned for the New Franc 1960; appears on French euro coins from 2002. Public domain (PD-FRANCE)."

### Fig. 3 — Semeuse stamp definitive (Lignée, 1903)

- **SCOUT ID:** FR-068 (série) or FR-069 (15 centimes specific)
- **Primary source:** La Poste / Musée de La Poste; or Colnect commons listing
- **URL:** https://colnect.com/en/stamps/list/country/74-France/series/68206 (corpus URL); but better-quality scans on Wikimedia Commons under "Stamps of France 1903"
- **Copyright:** PD-FRANCE; stamp engravings issued by the French State.
- **Proposed caption:** "Semeuse stamp, France, 1903. Adaptation of Roty's coin design by Louis-Eugène Mouchon. Endurecimento essentially unchanged across supports (coin 1898 / stamp 1903 / camée 1906) — the 'morphological stability' discussed in §3.2. Public domain (PD-FRANCE)."

### Fig. 4 — Germania Reichspost stamp (Waldraff), 1900

- **SCOUT ID:** DE-GERM-1900
- **Primary source:** Wikimedia Commons (multiple high-res scans); German Historical Museum (DHM)
- **URL:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germania_(stamp) (corpus); for figure use, https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:DR_1920_141_Germania.jpg (DE-017) or DE-021 commons.
- **Copyright:** PD-GERMANY (designer Waldraff d. 1917; 70-year rule expired 1987).
- **Proposed caption:** "10 Pfennig Germania definitive stamp, Deutsches Reich, designed by Paul Eduard Waldraff (1869–1917) using actress Anna Führing as model. First issued 1900; persisted through Weimar (Mi. 141, 1920) as discussed §4.3. Endurecimento 2.80 — among the highest in the corpus. Public domain (PD-GERMANY)."

### Fig. 5 — Notgeld Bielefeld, Jungbrunnen (Muggly), 1921

- **SCOUT ID:** DE-NOTG-1921 (and corpus duplicate DE-020 — to consolidate)
- **Primary source:** British Museum or University of Chicago Special Collections (footnote [^15] of WP names both)
- **URL:** corpus URL is internal-vault placeholder; the British Museum online collection has multiple Bielefeld silk Notgeld items: https://www.britishmuseum.org/collection (search "Bielefeld Notgeld silk")
- **Copyright:** PD-GERMANY (designer Karl Muggly d. before 1953, verify; safer route: derivative photograph from BM under CC-BY-NC for academic use)
- **Proposed caption:** "Notgeld, City of Bielefeld, 25 Mark on silk, issued 15 July 1921 for the city's 700th anniversary. Designer: Karl Muggly. The *Jungbrunnen* (Fountain of Youth) motif inverts every Germania indicator — see §4.4. Endurecimento 0.6, the lowest of the focal cases. The note circulated as local emergency money during the 1921–1923 hyperinflation. British Museum / University of Chicago Special Collections."

### Fig. 6 — Britannia Penny (Victoria, 1860 issue)

- **SCOUT ID:** UK-PENNY-1860
- **Primary source:** British Museum or Royal Mint Museum
- **URL:** https://en.numista.com/catalogue/pieces3377.html (corpus); for reproduction prefer https://www.britishmuseum.org/collection (search "penny Victoria Britannia") or open-access numismatic catalogues.
- **Copyright:** PD-UK (pre-1928 design; photograph of public-domain object generally PD or institutional CC-BY).
- **Proposed caption:** "Penny, United Kingdom, 1860 issue. Britannia *seated* with trident, helmet, and shield — the morphology held continuously since the late seventeenth century. Endurecimento 2.50 with dessexualization indicator at maximum (3); see §5 on the 'inertia of the already-hardened' body. Public domain (PD-UK)."

### Fig. 7 — 20.000 Réis Ouro, Efígie da República com Barrete Frígio, 1889

The 'positivist freeze' (§6) is inaugurated here: endurecimento at MEDIUM, dessexualization at maximum from inception. Public domain (PD-BRAZIL)."

### Fig. 8 — British Trade Dollar (De Saulles), 1895

- **SCOUT ID:** UK-TRADE-1895
- **Primary source:** British Museum (multiple specimens); Royal Mint historical archives
- **URL:** https://en.numista.com/catalogue/pieces8472.html (corpus); for reproduction prefer British Museum collection.
- **Copyright:** PD-UK; institutional photograph likely CC-BY.
- **Proposed caption:** "British Trade Dollar, 1895–1935. Designer: George William De Saulles. Obverse: Britannia *standing* on a colonial shore, trident raised, merchant ship in the background. Reverse: denomination in Chinese and Malay characters. Minted in Bombay, Calcutta, and London; circulated in Hong Kong and the Straits Settlements. Endurecimento 2.50, MILITARY regime; comparison with the seated domestic Britannia (Fig. 6) is the core evidence of §7. British Museum."
