## Framework in one line

Capital accumulation has two entangled sides — exchange of equivalents and expropriation — corresponding to two legal modes: fetishized form (Pashukanis; iconography) and explicit legal violence (Federici; codification). Fletcher, Portella & Busk, and Ince work the seam.

## Timeline lanes

1. Iconography (fetishized form) — Ripa 1593 → banknote allegories 1910
2. Codification — Enclosure (British General Enclosure Acts)
3. Codification — Labour (New Poor Law, Factory Acts, vagrancy)
4. Codification — Sexuality/Household (Code Napoléon, Infanticide Act, CD Acts)
5. Codification — Penal (Malleus 1487 → witch statutes → abortion criminalisation)
6. Codification — Colonial (India, Brazil) — CD Act 1868, Brazilian Penal Code 1890, Civil Code 1916
7. Counter-current — Louis XIV 1682, 1736 repeal, MWPA, CD Acts repeal, suffragist appropriation
