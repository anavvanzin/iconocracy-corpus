## Model Council Insights
The multi-model analysis reveals:
- **Consensus**: Focus on contestability, not just technology; Fernández González's critical vocabulary essential; blockchain as possible but not neutral; need for demonstrable proposals; digital inclusion as political validity criterion
- **Key Tensions**: Prototypical focus (contestation platform vs blockchain contracts); dramatic center (automated decision vs automated contract); blockchain role (secondary integrity vs primary demonstrative); audience focus (decision-affected advocates vs contract lawyers)

## Recommended Prototype
Based on model consensus: **GovLens/DueProcess.AI - Platform for Contestability of Administrative Public 4.0**
- Analyzes digital public decisions, identifies opacity, produces LAI/LGPD requests, suggests administrative appeals
- Evaluates discrimination risk and digital inclusion
- Includes smart contracts module showing blockchain only valid as integrity trace
